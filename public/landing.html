Problem Statement 2 Architecture

From “I don't know what to do” to “I know my next steps”

Kayda Sathi converts confusing legal jargon into a structured, actionable 5-pillar pathway for any everyday legal dispute in India.

⚖️
Pillar 1

Rights & Remedies

Identifies legal rights under statutory laws (e.g. Model Tenancy Act, Consumer Protection Act, Labor Code) in clear language.

📁
Pillar 2

Required Documents

Interactive, itemized checklist of evidence needed (rent agreements, bank transaction receipts, emails, police complaint copies).

🪜
Pillar 3

Suggested Next Steps

Prioritized step-by-step action plan: informal negotiation → legal notice → filing formal complaint → mediation.

🏛️
Pillar 4

Appropriate Authority

Directs citizens to the correct forum: Rent Authority, District Consumer Forum (NCDRC), Cyber Crime Portal, or NALSA Legal Aid.

✍️
Pillar 5

Customizable Complaint Draft

Generates ready-to-use formal letters (Legal Demand Notice, Formal Consumer Complaint, Police Application) that citizens can customize, copy, or print instantly.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kayda Sathi completely free for Indian citizens? ↓

Yes. Kayda Sathi is built to bridge the justice gap in India. All legal information, checklists, complaint templates, and authority directory tools are 100% free.

Can I speak in my own language instead of typing legal English? ↓

Yes! You can describe your problem in simple words or speak via voice in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, or English. The app translates your story into statutory legal requirements.

Does this replace an advocate or legal representation? ↓

Kayda Sathi provides clear legal information, procedural steps, and draft documents. For formal courtroom litigation, it connects you to NALSA Legal Aid Services and authorized legal aid clinics near you.
